# Archived results

`screening_rankings_100.csv` contains the report's full-period ranking, not an
independent holdout selection. `single_factor_forecasts_381.csv` contains 381
selected-model predictions. `window_forecasts_381x7.csv.gz` contains all three
training windows, their matched mean benchmarks and unchanged price.

`original_regression_*` is the 15 October 2026 historical example, not the final
November refit. Its JSON preserves earlier exploratory pair results and a then-
pending November status. `final_regression_evidence.json` and root
`final_forecast.json` contain the completed November forecast. These dated records
must not be combined as if generated at the same research stage.

`original_independent_verification.json` preserves the original audit receipt;
source paths and hashes refer to the original archive. The portable package has
its own `package_independent_verification.json`, generated by `verify_package.py`.
`package_manifest.json` records the checksums of packaged files at publication.
